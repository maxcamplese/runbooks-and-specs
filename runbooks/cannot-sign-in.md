# Runbook: Can't Sign In

**Who this is for:** anyone locked out of email, a work app, or their computer.
**Time:** about 5 minutes.

> **IT will never ask for your password.** Not by email, chat, or phone. If someone asks, do not give it. Report the message to IT.

---

## Step 1: Check the Basics

1. **Caps Lock** is off.
2. **Keyboard language** has not changed (look for "EN", "ES", or a flag in the menu bar or taskbar).
3. **You are using your work account**, not a personal one. Check the email address on the sign-in screen.
4. **You are on the real site.** Check the address bar. A fake sign-in page can look identical. If an email link brought you there, close it and go to the site directly.

## Step 2: Read the Message on the Screen

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Incorrect password" | Try once more, slowly. Then use **Forgot password** (Step 3). Too many tries can lock the account. |
| "Your account is locked" | Wait 15 to 30 minutes, or contact IT. Trying again does not help and can keep it locked longer. |
| "Your password has expired" | Follow the prompt to set a new one. |
| "You don't have access to this app" | Your account works, but you have not been given access. Contact IT or your manager (see below). |
| The code from your phone app does not work | Check that your phone's time is set automatically (Step 4). |
| No code arrives by text | Wait 2 minutes, check signal, then use **Try another way** or **Sign in another way** (the wording depends on the app), or a backup code if you have one. |

## Step 3: Reset Your Password

Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page. Choose a new password that:

- is **long** (a short sentence is easier to remember than a jumble of symbols),
- is **not used for any other account**,
- is saved in the company's password manager if you have one.

After you reset it, you may need to sign in again on your phone and other devices.

## Step 4: Fix Two-Factor (2FA) Codes

If the six-digit code from your authenticator app is rejected:

- **iPhone:** Settings > General > Date & Time > turn on **Set Automatically**.
- **Android:** Settings > System > Date & time > turn on **Set time automatically**.

The codes depend on the clock. If your phone's time is off by a minute, every code fails.

**New phone or lost phone?** Your codes did not move with you. Contact IT to reset two-factor. They will need to confirm who you are first. That is normal and protects your account.

---

## When to Contact IT

Contact IT if you are locked out, lost your two-factor device, or need access to a new app. Include:

1. **Which app or system** (for example "email," "Slack," or "my laptop login").
2. **The exact error message** or a screenshot.
3. **What you already tried** from this page.
4. For access requests: **what you need access to and why**, and who approved it (usually your manager).

**Never include your password** in the request.
